Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is AgriDrone?

AgriDrone is an app that allows farmers and agricultural professionals to monitor and control their drones for crop management, field monitoring, and other farming activities.

2. How do I connect my drone to the app?

To connect your drone, ensure that both your device and the drone are on the same Wi-Fi network. Follow the on-screen instructions in the app to pair your drone.

3. What should I do if my drone is not responding?

If your drone is not responding, ensure that the battery is charged, and check the Wi-Fi connection. If the issue persists, please contact our support team for further assistance.

4. How do I update the app?

You can update the app by visiting the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android) and downloading the latest version available.

5. How can I contact support?

If you need help, you can reach out to our support team through the "Contact Support" section in the app, or you can email us directly at support@agridrone.com.
